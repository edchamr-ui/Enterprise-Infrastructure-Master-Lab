## Business Description

Apex Manufacturing is a regional manufacturer supplying industrial equipment and engineering products throughout Southern Africa.

The company operates multiple production facilities, distribution centres and regional sales offices. Business operations rely heavily on secure communication between sites and high availability of business-critical applications.

# Current Business Challenges

The client has identified several infrastructure challenges:

- Ageing network infrastructure
- Limited redundancy
- Manual network administration
- Inadequate monitoring
- Limited disaster recovery capability
- Growing cybersecurity threats
- Rapid business expansion
- Increasing cloud adoption

---

# Business Objectives

The organisation intends to modernise its IT infrastructure to achieve the following objectives:

- Improve business continuity
- Increase network availability
- Enhance cybersecurity
- Standardise infrastructure
- Reduce operational risk
- Improve visibility and monitoring
- Support future business growth
- Enable automation
- Prepare for hybrid cloud deployment

---

# Availability Requirements

Critical business systems should maintain high availability with minimal downtime.

Target availability:

99.9% uptime

# Security Requirements

The infrastructure must provide:

- Network segmentation
- Role-based access control
- Secure remote access
- Multi-layer firewall protection
- Logging and auditing
- Secure authentication
- Least-privilege administration
- Continuous monitoring

# Future Growth

The infrastructure should be capable of supporting:

- Additional branch offices
- Cloud expansion
- Increased manufacturing capacity
- Remote workforce
- Additional enterprise applications

# Project Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- All business sites are interconnected
- Critical applications remain highly available
- Infrastructure is fully documented
- Monitoring and alerting are operational
- Security controls are implemented
- Disaster recovery procedures are validated
- Infrastructure automation is demonstrated
